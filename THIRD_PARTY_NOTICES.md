### Paragraph context model

`scout/models/paragraph_context.json` contains logistic-regression parameters
for paragraph language (EN / PL / mixed) and document style. It was trained for
this project on 840 synthetic paragraphs generated by Gemini 4 Hydrogen
(600 typed EN/PL/mixed paragraphs and 240 English paragraphs; SHA-256 of the two
training files `9d011aa0…` and `1460d831…`), with no external dataset text, and
is distributed under the plugin's MIT license. The file holds only coefficients,
no training text. It is not derived from OEWN. Classification can fail.

## Dependencies and optional models

### English name models C and G

The `name_c_span.json` and `name_g_slot.json` artifacts use bounded English training samples from
[NVIDIA Nemotron-PII](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Nemotron-PII),
NVIDIA Corporation, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/);
[Gretel synthetic_pii_finance_multilingual](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gretelai/synthetic_pii_finance_multilingual),
Gretel, Apache License 2.0; and
[WNUT 2017 Emerging Entities](https://github.com/leondz/emerging_entities_17),
Leon Derczynski and contributors, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
WNUT reference: Derczynski et al., *Results of the WNUT2017 Shared Task on Novel
and Emerging Entity Recognition* (2017). Entity offsets were mapped to the
plugin's full-person span convention; WNUT tokens were joined with spaces.
These models also use the common name-training data attributed above.
C supplies multi-word proposals and person seed evidence; G supplies grammar
slot scores and inherits the F2 confirmed-propagation mechanism. The shared F implementation is shipped as a dependency of G; experimental A/A2/B and F model weights are not shipped.
Dataset licenses were checked in the locally supplied dataset cards and
attribution README on 2026-11-13; no source texts are distributed in the plugin.

### English grammar and animacy data

`scout/models/name_g_lexicon.json` is an adapted, vocabulary-limited extract of
Open English WordNet 2025, by **The Open English WordNet Team**, copyright
(c) 2019-present. Source: https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet,
`2025-edition`, `english-wordnet-2025-json.zip`.
Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The data are provided without
warranties; no endorsement by the authors is implied.

Changes: only ordinary lowercase, single-token lemmas observed in training or
in the existing ordinary-frequency vocabulary are retained. Capitalized lemmas
and senses whose synset has `instance_hypernym` are excluded. Only counts by
part of speech and lexicographer group, plus ordinary inflected forms, remain;
definitions, examples and proper-name entries are not distributed. The supplied
2025 JSON contains no `instance_hypernym` fields. The G coefficients use these
features and the same attributed training sources as C and the shared F2 mechanism; no new
person-name list is supplied.

`scout/models/name_nationality.json` is a separate adapted extract of OEWN 2025
`adj.pert` under the same attribution and licenses. It retains 280 single-token,
capitalized ethnogeographic adjectives (lowercased for lookup) and their synset
IDs where the definition refers to its/their people, inhabitants, culture or
language. Definitions and examples are omitted. This class can include regional
and cultural adjectives as well as nationalities; it contains no person,
company or work gazetteer. Source file SHA-256 is included in the artifact.
